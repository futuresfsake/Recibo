The receipt system

Resibo means receipt. The interface is built like one: paper, ink, a perforated edge, a serial number, and stamps. The look comes from the idea, so it does not need decoration.

Rules this system follows

  • Every claim sits next to its quote. A claim with no verified quote shows as a rejected line, not as a claim.
  • Meaning is never carried by color alone: marks have shapes and labels.
  • Touch targets are at least 44px. Text is at least 12px. Layout holds at 360px.
  • One accent per job: blue means proof, red means rejected or a serial. Nothing else is colored.
  • Rounded corners are 2px. The receipt edge is the only decoration.

What it avoids on purpose

  • Gradient washes, glass panels, glowing shadows, purple-to-blue anything.
  • Grids of identical rounded cards with an icon in a circle.
  • Emoji as icons, sparkle marks, and "AI" badges.
  • Stock hero copy. Every line of text says something specific to this product.
