Rules this system follows

  • Every claim sits next to its quote. A claim with no verified quote shows as a rejected line, not as a claim.
  • Meaning is never carried by color alone: marks have shapes and glyphs, results have words.
  • Touch targets are at least 44px. Text is at least 12px. Layout holds at 360px.
  • One accent. Green means verified, red means rejected, amber means a signal or partial match. Nothing else is colored.
  • Hairline borders and one soft shadow. No gradients, glass or glow.
